A free tool from SONJARZO AI that turns your words into cut-paper letter art: each letter on its own torn paper tile, layered with vintage pages and gold leaf.

What it's for

Quote cards, social posts, wall art, greeting cards and banners. Use it for a single word like Grateful or Joy, a name, a theme, or a short message for an event, a holiday or your business.

How to use it
Type your words (up to 20 letters).
Add a symbol tile if you like.
Pick your colors, shape and where it will live.
Tap Build my prompt, copy it, and paste it into ChatGPT, Gemini or Midjourney.
