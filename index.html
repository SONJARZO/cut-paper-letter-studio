Cut-Paper Letter Studio

Turn your words into art made of cut-paper letter tiles, torn vintage pages and gold leaf. Type a word or short phrase, pick your colors, and copy a finished prompt for ChatGPT, Gemini or Midjourney.

What it's for

Quote cards, social posts, wall art, greeting cards and banners. Use it for a single word like Grateful or Joy, a name, a theme, or a short message for an event, a holiday or your business.

How to use it

  1. Type your words (up to 20 letters).
  2. Add a symbol tile if you like.
  3. Pick your colors, shape and where it will live.
  4. Tap Build my prompt, copy it, and paste it into ChatGPT, Gemini or Midjourney.

What to do with your prompt

  1. Pick the tab for the tool you use and tap Copy.
  2. Paste it into a new chat.
  3. If a letter comes out wrong, reply: "Fix the tile that should say ___ and keep everything else the same."

    

    
Midjourney often misspells or scrambles letters, and cut-paper tiles are the hardest style for it. Expect to run it a few times, or use ChatGPT or Gemini.
